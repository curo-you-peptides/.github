## Curo Peptides

Curo ([curo.you](https://curo.you)) sells research peptides. Each lot has a third-party lab report (COA).

### Curo Peptide Research MCP

[peptide-research-mcp](https://github.com/curo-you-peptides/peptide-research-mcp) connects your AI to peptide research and to the Curo catalog. Your AI can answer peptide questions from [PepBank](https://pepbank.io) study findings, and look up Curo peptides with their lab reports, prices and stock.

Server address: `https://mcp.curo.you`. Setup guide: [curo.you/mcp](https://curo.you/mcp).
